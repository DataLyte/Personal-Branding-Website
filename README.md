**What is this project?**

This project is a professional personal website that presents my skills, experience, projects and professional interests in a way that demonstrates my value to potential employers and organisations.

**Why did I create this project?**

The website was created to act as a professional portfolio and personal brand platform. Its primary goal is not simply to display information about me, but to provide clear evidence of my capabilities and encourage employers, recruiters and organisations to contact me regarding employment, interviews, networking opportunities or other professional opportunities.

**What technologies have I used?**

The technologies I have used is Wix Studio to build the wireframe, Microsoft Word for documentation and building out the Project Plan. 

**Any features you would like to add in the future?**

In the Hero section I would add a short list of tools/logos (Power BI, Excel, SQL if applicable) right in the hero so recruiters skimming get an instant skills signal before scrolling.

Adding a sticky nav bar so a busy recruiter can jump straight to what they care about instead of scrolling through everything. This is probably the single highest-impact addition.

The outcomes are currently qualitative, where I can quantify my achievements, I should include it in the website i.e. Did the OPEX dashboard identify a specific £ amount of overspend? Did the NYC breakdown analysis suggest a specific % reduction potential? 

Project contrasts are low quality and hard to read. The dashboard screenshots (Finance Dashboard, Croydon West chart) are small and somewhat washed out against the grey panel. Larger high resolution screenshots or an actual embedded interactive Power BI/Tableau view if the platform supports it.

Adding a direct project link rather than just a styled button with no visible destination — some users hesitate to click ambiguous buttons.

Work History section is text heavy. I could add company logos next to each role for visual anchoring and scannability. Consider collapsible/expandable sections per role so the page doesn't feel like a CV dump. Bold the outcome words within each bullet (e.g. zero missed deadlines, £1500 budget) to help skimmers. 

Contact section is very bare. I can add clickable mailto: and LinkedIn buttons/icons. Optionally, a short contact form so people don't need to leave the site to reach you. Consider adding your CV download here too, as a second touchpoint. 

Missing About section. There's no dedicated "About Me" or education section — degree, university, relevant coursework. Employers will want quick context on your academic background alongside the project work.
