## Project Overview

### What is this project?

This project is a professional personal website that presents my skills, experience, projects and professional interests in a way that demonstrates my value to potential employers and organisations.

The website provides a central platform for showcasing my professional background, technical capabilities and practical experience through completed projects and previous employment.

## Project Purpose

### Why did I create this project?

The website was created to act as a professional portfolio and personal brand platform.

Its primary goal is not simply to display information about me, but to provide clear evidence of my capabilities and encourage employers, recruiters and organisations to contact me regarding:

* Employment opportunities
* Job interviews
* Professional networking
* Other professional opportunities

### Featured Projects

The website currently showcases three data analytics projects.

**1. OPEX Finance Dashboard**

A Power BI dashboard designed to provide visibility into operational expenditure and support budget monitoring and financial decision-making.

**2. Croydon West Data Analysis**

An Excel-based analysis comparing socioeconomic conditions in Croydon West with other constituencies to support evidence-based recommendations.

**3. NYC Bus Breakdown Analysis**

An analysis of more than 282,000 New York City bus incident records to identify patterns in breakdowns, delays and service disruptions.

## Future Improvements

### What features would I like to add in the future?

The following improvements have been identified to enhance the website's usability, presentation and effectiveness in communicating my professional capabilities.

### 1. Hero Section

**Improvement: Skills and Tools Visibility**

In the Hero section, I would add a short list of tools or logos, such as Power BI, Excel and SQL (if applicable).

This would give recruiters an immediate indication of my technical capabilities before scrolling through the website.

### 2. Navigation

**Improvement: Sticky Navigation Bar**

Adding a sticky navigation bar would allow busy recruiters to jump directly to the sections that interest them rather than scrolling through the entire website.

This is potentially one of the highest-impact improvements for usability.

### 3. Project Outcomes

**Improvement: Quantifiable Achievements**

Where project outcomes are currently qualitative, I would aim to quantify achievements to demonstrate their measurable impact.

Examples include:

* **OPEX Finance Dashboard:** Did the dashboard identify a specific £ amount of overspend?
* **NYC Bus Breakdown Analysis:** Did the analysis suggest a specific percentage reduction potential?

Including measurable outcomes would provide stronger evidence of the value delivered by each project.

### 4. Project Visuals and Presentation

**Improvement: Higher-Quality Project Screenshots**

The contrast and quality of some project visuals could be improved.

The dashboard screenshots, including the Finance Dashboard and Croydon West chart, are relatively small and appear washed out against the grey background panels.

Potential improvements include:

* Using larger, higher-resolution screenshots.
* Improving contrast between dashboard screenshots and their backgrounds.
* Embedding interactive Power BI or Tableau dashboards where supported by the platform.

### 5. Project Accessibility

**Improvement: Clear Project Links**

Adding direct, clearly labelled project links would improve accessibility.

Some users may hesitate to click buttons when their destinations are unclear.

The website should consistently provide identifiable links to supporting project files, dashboards or other relevant resources.

### 6. Professional Experience

**Improvement: Work History Presentation**

The Work History section is text-heavy and could benefit from a more visually structured layout.

Potential improvements include:

* Adding company logos next to each role for visual anchoring.
* Introducing collapsible or expandable sections to reduce the amount of visible text.
* Highlighting measurable achievements within each role.

For example:

* **Zero missed deadlines**
* **£1,500 budget**

These changes would make professional achievements easier to identify when recruiters skim the website.

### 7. Contact Section

**Improvement: Contact Accessibility and Engagement**

The Contact section could benefit from additional functionality and improved presentation.

Potential improvements include:

* Making email and LinkedIn contact options more visually prominent.
* Adding a short contact form so visitors can get in touch without leaving the website.
* Providing a downloadable CV within the Contact section as an additional access point.

### 8. About Me and Education

**Improvement: Academic Background**

Although the website includes an About Me section, it could provide more detailed information about my academic background.

Information to include:

* Degree and academic qualifications.
* University attended.
* Relevant coursework.

This would provide employers with additional context about my educational background alongside my practical project work.
